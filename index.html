About Me

I’m a Computational Media Masters student at UC Santa Cruz. I am interested in game design, player research, UI/UX design, and so much more!

Most of my experience comes from team-based game projects, where I’ve taken on roles including design, UI, organization, and project presentation/ pitching. I really enjoy figuring out how all of the moving parts of a project fit together, and I’ve learned a lot through these collaborative projects.

Outside of game development, I've also worked on research involving large language models and procedural content generation, as well as developing a product search tool during my internship.

Currently, I am working on UI design for a map generator that uses Wave Function Collapse!
