About

MageTender is a 2D pixel art fantasy bartending game where players craft drinks to guide a customer’s mood toward a target outcome. Each ingredient shifts emotional values, turning the drink-making process into a small strategy puzzle.

Trailer

Each customer enters with a current mood and a desired state. After a short interaction, the player mixes ingredients that push those values in different directions, aiming to get as close as possible to the target with a single drink.

During development, I focused on UI and marketing. Through playtesting, we found that players understood the goal but struggled to interpret how their choices affected the result. To solve this issue, I designed and iterated on mood graph UI that clearly displays both current and target states, making the system more readable, especially for new players.

I also created content to promote the game across various platforms, including YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. I'm proud to say that we were able to get our wishlist count into the hundreds!

Takeaways

Through this project, I’ve learned the importance of consistent playtesting, and using player feedback to guide design decisions early and often. Working within a larger team has had its own learning curve as well, but through weekly standups and well-structured meetings, we were able to release the game on Steam.
