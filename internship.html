Product Search Application for Coherent Corp

I worked on this project during my Digital Marketing Internship at Coherent Corp. For my primary internship project, I was tasked with creating an application that would facilitate matching compatible cables and transceivers, making the process more efficient for customers and technicians.

Application Demo

I developed the application using Flutter and Firebase in order for it to be compatible with both Apple and Android devices. The app was required to have filters such as speed, form factor, reach, connector, etc. Moreover, it needed a save feature to bookmark relevant products, as well as an easy way to add future products through the backend. Additionally, I needed to collect all of the data from various sources around the company, which allowed me to work with different teams to better understand the products.

This project was a great opportunity to learn new technologies and gain experience developing an application in a professional environment.
